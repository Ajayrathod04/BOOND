TRIAGE PRIORITY FORMULA (HEURISTIC AID)

Priority Score = Flow Midpoint × Drinking Multiplier (1.5x) × Reporter Multiplier

Midpoints: Drip (125 L/d) · Steady (1,250 L/d) · Gush (9,500 L/d). Reporter multiplier adds +0.25 per additional citizen report.

TRIAGE AID DISCLAIMER

Priority ranking is a triage aid for maintenance prioritization, not an official municipal order or a verified physical measurement of actual water saved. Seeded reports are simulated for demonstration.

HOW THE NUMBERS WORK (METHODOLOGY & HONESTY)

1. Flow Rates & Heuristic Priority

Flows are estimated: Drip (50–200 L/d, midpoint 125), Steady (500–2,000 L/d, midpoint 1,250), and Gush (4,000–15,000 L/d, midpoint 9,500). Priority formula applies a 1.5x multiplier for drinking water lines and +0.25 per additional reporter.

2. 30-Metre Proximity Merging

New reports within 30 metres of an existing open leak are automatically merged using the Haversine formula. The reporter count increases and the flow severity upgrades to the higher value observed.

3. Cedar Policy Authorization

Ticket closures require authenticated engineer status and verified after-repair photo proof. Reopening closed tickets requires at least 2 citizen disputes. Authorization decisions fail closed by design.

4. Honest Estimation Disclaimer

Estimated leakage addressed represents triage ranking metrics, NOT verified water saved. Demo seed tickets are simulated and never claimed as live reports. Prototype PIN authentication must be replaced with Cognito in production.
